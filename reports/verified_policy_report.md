**EXECUTIVE POLICY DIRECTIVE**

**FOR THE MUNICIPAL COMMISSIONER, CHHATRAPATI SAMBHAJINAGAR**

**SUBJECT: Strengthening Education Infrastructure and Policy Implementation**

**EXECUTIVE DIAGNOSTIC & BOTTLENECK ANALYSIS**

The district's education system faces a critical bottleneck in the transition from Upper Primary to Secondary education, with a significant drop-off of 29,497 students (76,819 - 47,322). This is exacerbated by the backlog of 27,235 pending promotion students, posing a substantial administrative and infrastructure risk. The computed Infrastructure Readiness Score of 88.1/100 indicates a need for targeted interventions to enhance infrastructure and teaching capacity.

**TARGETED STATUTORY DIRECTIVES**

To address these challenges, we recommend the following three binding directives:

**Directive A: Remedial & Retention**

* Cite Samagra Shiksha remedial bridge frameworks for the 27,235 non-promoted students to ensure timely promotion and reduce the backlog.
* Implement targeted remedial programs, including:
	+ Bridge courses for non-promoted students
	+ Intensive teaching and learning support
	+ Regular monitoring and feedback mechanisms

**Directive B: Secondary Expansion**

* Cite NEP 2020 universal secondary access and school complex consolidation norms to ensure all students have access to secondary education.
* Implement:
	+ Classroom expansion and upgrading of existing schools
	+ Consolidation of school complexes to enhance resource sharing and efficiency
	+ Strengthening of teacher training and capacity building programs

**Directive C: Gender Equity & Infrastructure**

* Cite RTE Act norms for dedicated girl-student sanitation and safety infrastructure to ensure a safe and inclusive learning environment.
* Implement:
	+ Construction of dedicated girl-student toilets and sanitation facilities
	+ Installation of safety and security measures, including CCTV cameras and emergency response systems
	+ Training of teachers and staff on gender sensitivity and inclusive education practices

**PHASED IMPLEMENTATION ROADMAP**

**Immediate Action (0–6 Months)**

* Conduct target audits to identify areas of improvement
* Launch remedial bridge programs for non-promoted students
* Establish a monitoring and feedback mechanism to track progress

**Medium-Term Plan (6–24 Months)**

* Implement classroom expansion and upgrading of existing schools
* Consolidate school complexes to enhance resource sharing and efficiency
* Strengthen teacher training and capacity building programs

**2037 Vision Target: Long-range Demographic Capacity Readiness**

* Ensure all schools have adequate infrastructure and teaching capacity to meet the needs of the growing student population
* Implement a robust monitoring and evaluation system to track progress and identify areas for improvement
* Continuously review and update policies and programs to ensure alignment with emerging needs and best practices

**CONCLUSION**

The Municipal Commissioner is urged to prioritize the implementation of these directives to strengthen education infrastructure and policy implementation in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar. By addressing the critical bottlenecks in the education system, we can ensure a more inclusive, equitable, and effective education system for all students.
